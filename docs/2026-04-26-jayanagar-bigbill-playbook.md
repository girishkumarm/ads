## Pricing — birthday + corporate packages

### Birthday tiers
| Tier | Pax / Hours | Price (incl GST) | Per head | Differentiator |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|
| Casual | 10 / 2 hr | 5,999 | 600 | Reserved corner, balloon arch, 1kg cake, shared server, Rs 500 cake-cutting fee |
| **Standard "Most Booked"** | 15 / 3 hr | 9,999 | 667 | Half upper floor, theme backdrop, 2kg cake, 1 dedicated server, 30 photos, free outside cake |
| Premium | 25 / 4 hr | 19,999 | 800 | **Full private upper floor**, full theme + LED + entry frame, 3kg designer cake + sparklers, 2 servers + host, 100 photos + 5-min reel |

Anchor: lead menu page with Premium → Standard reads "save Rs 10k". 80% choose Standard. Decoy: Casual = same per-head food, no private space.

Deposit 50% UPI on booking. 7+ days 100% refund · 3-6 days 50% · <48h forfeit (50% credit note 90d valid).

### Corporate tiers (per pax incl GST, min 10 pax)
- **Veg Lite Rs 350** — Essential thali, 24h notice
- **Veg Standard Rs 500** — +2 starters +3 mains +raita +2 desserts, 48h notice
- **Premium + Bev Rs 750** — +soup +live counter +filter coffee/mocktails, 72h notice

Free delivery <5km on orders ≥ Rs 4,000. Single GST invoice. First 3 orders 100% advance UPI. 4th+ = 7-day credit. 30-day credit only after Rs 50k cumulative + signed PO.

### Off-peak fill (huge for empty hours)
**Mon/Tue/Wed 2-5 PM: 15% off all packages.** Branded "Midweek Mellow". Excludes Fri-Sun and Dec.

**First big-bill booking goes badly** (slow service, decor mismatch, food delay) → 1-star review on a 4.4★ base → kills LP conversion rate for 60+ days.

1. Girish personally on-floor for first 3 birthday + 2 corporate bookings
2. Pre-event checklist: decor 2hr early, dedicated server, kitchen prep brief T-3-day, photo handoff plan
3. Host-feedback call within 24hr of event
4. Review-request ONLY after positive feedback confirmed
5. Public liability insurance bound BEFORE first booking — slip/fall claims close cafes
