**A:** Yes, we have covered outdoor seating for 12 guests. Indoor AC seating for 40+ guests including our quiet upper floor.

**Q5:** Do you accept UPI and cards?
**A:** Yes — UPI, all major debit/credit cards, and cash. Tap-to-pay NFC enabled.

**Q6:** Is BUS Cafe good for a date?
**A:** Yes — our upper floor has window seats, warm lighting after 6 PM, and a quieter ambience than most Jayanagar cafes. Couples' combo available weekday evenings.

**Q7:** Can I do a surprise birthday or anniversary here?
**A:** Yes — we've hosted 20+ surprises and anniversaries. Call us 24 hours ahead and we'll help arrange decor, a cake, and the quieter corner table.

**Q8:** Can I book BUS Cafe for a birthday party?
**A:** Yes — we take birthday bookings for 6 to 30 guests. Private upper floor available. Outside cake allowed (no cake-cutting fee). Call or book via Zomato/EazyDiner 48 hours ahead.

**Q9:** Do you have a private dining room?
**A:** Our upper floor can be booked privately for groups of 15+ with advance notice. Minimum spend applies on weekends.

**Q10:** Do you charge a cake-cutting fee?
**A:** No cake-cutting fee. Bring your own cake or order from our bakery partner. We provide plates, knife, and candles free of cost.

**Q11:** Can I decorate for a birthday?
**A:** Yes — basic decor (balloons, table confetti) allowed. For heavy setup please arrive 1 hour before booking. We also offer in-house decor packages starting Rs 1,500.

### Work & Laptop

**Q12:** Is BUS Cafe laptop-friendly? Do you have Wi-Fi?
**A:** Yes — free high-speed Wi-Fi (150 Mbps), power sockets at 80% of tables, and a dedicated quiet zone on the upper floor. No time limit if you're ordering.

**Q13:** Can I work here for a few hours?
**A:** Yes, all day. Most regulars work 2–4 hours. We only ask you to order something every 90 minutes to keep things fair for other guests.

**Q14:** Is it too noisy to take video calls?
**A:** The upper floor is designed for this — quiet, good lighting, and calls are welcome. Ground floor gets busy during 12–2 PM lunch rush.

**Q15:** Do you have AC?
**A:** Yes — indoor seating is fully air-conditioned, both ground floor and upper floor. Outdoor covered seating has fans.
