## Why Previous Campaigns Were Paused

| Campaign | Issue |
|----------|-------|
| JNR Video Ads | High CTR (6.86%), Rs 0.19 CPC — but traffic objective. No call/visit conversion tracking. |
| JNR Hyper Local | Frequency rising (1.72), small audience, creative getting stale. |
| JNR Best of Bangalore | Low CTR (1.22%), high CPM — generic awareness creative not converting. |

**Root cause:** All campaigns used OUTCOME_TRAFFIC objective → optimized for clicks, not footfall or orders. People clicked, saw the page, left. No conversion event to optimize toward.

**Fix:** New campaigns will use either OUTCOME_LEADS (for calls/WhatsApp) or OUTCOME_TRAFFIC optimized for LANDING_PAGE_VIEWS with strong CTA destination — Zomato/Swiggy for orders, phone for bookings.

## Demographic Baseline (From JNR Video Ads — 30 days)

**Best performing segments:**
- 25-34 Female: 8.95% CTR — **top performer**
- 25-34 Male: 7.21% CTR
- 18-24 Female: 6.64% CTR
- 18-24 Male: 5.85% CTR
- 35-44: CTR ~6-7% but smaller volume

**Key insight:** Women 25-34 are the top converting demographic — likely driving date nights, cafe meetups, and WFC decisions. All campaigns should have female-weighted creative and this segment in primary audience.

## UTM Parameters (for all new campaigns)

Every ad destination URL should include UTM tracking:

```
Zomato/Maps/WhatsApp links:
?utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=paid_social&utm_campaign=[CAMPAIGN_SLUG]&utm_content=[AD_CREATIVE_SLUG]

Examples:
- WFC: utm_campaign=jnr_wfc&utm_content=unlimited_coffee_349
- Orders: utm_campaign=jnr_orders&utm_content=combo_499
- Events: utm_campaign=jnr_events&utm_content=birthday_booking
- Date Night: utm_campaign=jnr_date_night&utm_content=bogo_ice_cream
```

## Conversion Tracking Setup (CRITICAL — Do Before Launch)

The previous campaigns failed to show footfall because conversion events weren't set up. Before launching these campaigns:

1. **WhatsApp click tracking** — Use a WhatsApp Business link with UTM; FB can track button clicks as a conversion event.
2. **Phone call tracking** — Set up FB's "Call Now" objective to track calls as conversions.
3. **Zomato/Swiggy** — These are third-party apps; FB can only track the click to the app/website. Set "link click" as the conversion proxy and measure weekly order counts manually.
4. **Google Maps** — Track "Get Directions" clicks as a conversion event in FB pixel (if pixel on cafe website).

**Recommended conversion hierarchy:**
- Campaign 1 (WFC): Phone call / WhatsApp message
- Campaign 2 (Orders): Landing page view on Zomato/Swiggy
- Campaign 3 (Events): WhatsApp message
- Campaign 4 (Date Night): Get Directions click
- Campaign 5 (Students): Get Directions click
- Campaign 6 (Retarget): Phone call / WhatsApp message
